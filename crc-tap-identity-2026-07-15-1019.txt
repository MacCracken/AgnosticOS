  silence  source=0 :    RESULT = 0x00000001  ->  CRC_DONE=1  CRC=0x000000  (ZERO -- samples are digital silence)
  silence  source=1 :    RESULT = 0x00000001  ->  CRC_DONE=1  CRC=0x000000  (ZERO -- samples are digital silence)

=== CASE B: 440 Hz TONE playing ===
  tone     source=0 :    RESULT = 0x80fd3801  ->  CRC_DONE=1  CRC=0x80fd38
  tone     source=1 :    RESULT = 0x15f00201  ->  CRC_DONE=1  CRC=0x15f002

=== HOW TO READ THIS ===
  tap 0 CRC DIFFERS between silence and tone
      -> tap 0 is sample-content-sensitive. agnos's 0x25EB75 means REAL SAMPLES reach its AFMT, the
         zeroing is genuinely between the taps, and the Azalia endpoint diff (CONVERTER_FORMAT,
         AUDIO_DESCRIPTOR0, SINK_INFO0, MULTICHANNEL_MODE, verb 0x789) is EXONERATED — all of it sits
         upstream of both taps. Hunt moves inside the AFMT.

  tap 0 CRC IDENTICAL (and non-zero) for silence and tone
      -> tap 0 reads IEC-60958 FRAMING, not PCM. agnos's "content at tap 0" was an ECHO of the 60958
         channel-status registers, its samples are zero AT THE SOURCE, and every endpoint register agnos
         never writes is back in scope.

  Either way: half the search space dies, for zero burns.
