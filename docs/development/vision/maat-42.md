## What This Means

The 42 Negative Confessions are not a moral checklist. They are a **knowledge completeness test**. The soul must demonstrate awareness of every domain of existence — from physics (#35, flow of water) to psychology (#13, eating the heart) to economics (#6, purloined offerings) to sound (#36, raised voice) to cosmic order (#7, property of God).

Ma'at — Thoth's consort, Lord of Order — designed the test. Thoth — the Librarian — built the library to pass it.

AGNOS passes 42 for 42. Not because the crates were designed to match the confessions, but because a complete library of human knowledge naturally covers the same domains that a 4,000-year-old completeness test identified.

The confessions and the crate list were created independently, millennia apart. They converge because they're both trying to do the same thing: catalog everything that matters.

The Librarian passes Ma'at's judgment. The library is complete.

## Remaining Domain

#26 deserves elaboration. "I have not shut my ears to the words of truth" maps to three crates because truth has three forms:

- **shabdakosh** (v1.1.0) — the words themselves, correctly heard and pronounced. The treasury of words.
- **jnana** (v0.5.0) — unified knowledge system. The corpus of truth, queryable and accessible.
- **itihas** (v1.0.1) — recorded history. The truth of what actually happened, preserved across time.

To shut your ears to truth is to fail at all three: not hearing the words correctly (shabdakosh), not seeking knowledge (jnana), not preserving what happened (itihas).
